How it works

One calm path from
brief to handoff.

Desk is intentionally small. It covers the admin that actually moves money and delivery — without turning you into an operations department.

01

Win the work

Keep clients and projects in one place. Build proposals with line items, due dates, and statuses that stay honest — Draft, Sent, Accepted, or Declined.

02

Get paid

Turn a yes into an invoice with the same line items. Export a PDF, copy a paste-ready note for chat, and mark it Paid when the money lands.

03

Deliver cleanly

Package filenames and handoff notes into a read-only delivery portal. Share the link; the client can open it without an account.

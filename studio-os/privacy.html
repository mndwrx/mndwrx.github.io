Privacy policy

Studio OS Desk · Last updated Oct 2, 2026 · Operator: MNDWRX

Short version

Desk is local-first: your clients, proposals, invoices, and delivery notes are stored in your browser on your computer unless you export/back them up or paste them elsewhere. We do not run a Studio OS cloud database for your desk records today. Buying uses Lemon Squeezy; visiting the sales site uses GitHub Pages (and currently Google Fonts). We do not run marketing analytics on the sales page.

What we collect directly

  • Support email you send us (name, email, message, order ID if you include it) at support@mndwrx.com.
  • Nothing from inside the Desk app over the network today — no app telemetry or analytics in current builds.

What third parties may process

  • Lemon Squeezy — at checkout, invoices, refunds, and license emails: name, email, payment metadata, tax region, IP, order contents (payment, fraud, tax, delivery of digital goods).
  • GitHub Pages — when you visit mndwrx.github.io/studio-os: IP, user-agent, and request logs per GitHub (to host the static sales/app files).
  • Google Fonts — when sales/app CSS imports fonts: IP / request to Google servers for font files. We plan to self-host fonts later.
  • Future license check — only if/when enabled: license key, coarse device/app version, IP to our or Lemon’s validator (confirm purchase, revoke after refund).

Cookies & tracking

  • Studio OS does not use marketing analytics cookies on the current sales page.
  • Your browser will store local site data for the Desk (localStorage) if you use the app.
  • Third parties above may set their own cookies on their domains (especially Lemon checkout).

Local data on your machine

Stored under browser keys such as studio-os-desk-v1, studio-os-license-v1, and studio-os-theme-v1.

  • Backup: use in-app Backup to download JSON; Restore to load it.
  • Wipe: clear this site’s data in your browser settings (and delete backup files you made).
  • Clearing site data deletes local desk records — back up first.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not put your client list on our servers (there is no Desk cloud sync today).

Retention

  • Support emails: keep as long as needed to help you and meet bookkeeping/legal needs.
  • Lemon retains purchase records per their policies.
  • Local desk data retention is your choice on your device.

Your choices

  • Don’t use checkout if you don’t want to share purchase data with Lemon.
  • Self-host or block Google Fonts if you don’t want that request (we plan to self-host).
  • Export then wipe local data anytime.

Children

Not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where relevant); we don’t knowingly sell to kids.

Contact & changes

Privacy / support: support@mndwrx.com · America/Chicago. We’ll update this page if the product starts phoning home (license checks, analytics, etc.) and aim to say so on the sales page too.
